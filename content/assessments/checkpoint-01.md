# Checkpoint 01 · Chapters 1–3

**Status: complete course draft for Chapters 1–3.** This non-certified self-assessment reviews L01–L09. Use the topic profile to choose revision lessons; it does not award an official CEFR level.

## A. Grammar and vocabulary

Answer without notes.

1. Complete: *Wir ___ heute in der Stadt.* (sein)
2. Complete: *Du ___ deine Schwester.* (haben)
3. Give the definite article and plural: *Buchhandlung*.
4. Complete: *Das sind ___ Schlüssel.* (my)
5. Put in order: **morgen / Mara / arbeiten / in der Buchhandlung**.
6. Make a W-question asking where Lea studies.
7. Negate the noun phrase: *Ich habe eine Karte.*
8. Negate the adjective: *Der Weg ist weit.*
9. Identify the subject: *Heute arbeitet meine Schwester im Café.*
10. Correct: *Ihr ist heute nicht im Büro.* (meaning: “You [plural] are not in the office today.”)

<details><summary>Show solutions and explanations</summary><ol><li>sind — *wir* form of *sein*.</li><li>hast — *du* form of *haben*.</li><li>die Buchhandlung; die Buchhandlungen. Feminine noun; plural adds -en.</li><li>meine — plural possessive agrees with *Schlüssel*.</li><li>Morgen arbeitet Mara in der Buchhandlung. Fronted time phrase, finite verb second, subject after it.</li><li>Wo studiert Lea? W-word first, finite verb second.</li><li>Ich habe keine Karte. *Kein* negates an indefinite noun phrase.</li><li>Der Weg ist nicht weit. *Nicht* negates the adjective.</li><li>*Meine Schwester* is the subject; it agrees with *arbeitet*.</li><li>Ihr seid heute nicht im Büro. The *ihr* form of *sein* is *seid*.</li></ol></details>

11. When is Karim free? 12. Where does he meet his friend? 13. Who has two concert tickets? 14. Is the friend holding a ticket? Give the evidence.

## D. Chapter 2 case review (L04–L06)

Complete each sentence with the correct article or pronoun. Then explain whether the highlighted role is the direct object or recipient.

17. Ich kaufe ___ Mantel. (*der Mantel*) 18. Wir sehen ___ Verkäuferin. (*die Verkäuferin*) 19. Er hilft ___ Nachbarin. (*die Nachbarin*) 20. Das Geschenk gehört ___ Kindern. (*die Kinder*) 21. Kannst du ___ bitte anrufen? (*ich*) 22. Ich gebe ___ Schlüssel. (*du*, recipient; *der Schlüssel*, thing) 23. Sie schickt ___ die Fotos. (*ihr*, recipient; *die Fotos*, thing) 24. Heute besucht ___ Arzt den Patienten. (*der Arzt*, subject)

25. Put in neutral order: *Ich erkläre (es / dem Gast).* 26. Put in neutral order: *Wir schicken (dem Chef / die Nachricht).* 27. Correct the case error: *Ich danke meinen Bruder.* 28. Correct the case error: *Sie gibt der Kollegin.* (meaning: “She gives the colleague the form.”)

<details><summary>Show answers and explanations</summary><ol><li>den — masculine accusative article changes from *der* to *den*.</li><li>die — feminine accusative looks like nominative.</li><li>der — *helfen* governs dative; feminine dative is *der*.</li><li>den — dative plural uses *den* and usually adds -n to the noun: *Kindern*.</li><li>mich — *anrufen* takes an accusative object.</li><li>Ich gebe dir den Schlüssel. Recipient *dir* is dative; thing *den Schlüssel* is accusative. With two noun phrases, dative commonly precedes accusative.</li><li>Sie schickt ihr die Fotos. Dative recipient comes before the full accusative noun phrase.</li><li>Der Arzt — subject is nominative despite its position after *Heute*; the finite verb remains second.</li><li>Ich erkläre es dem Gast. A pronoun precedes a full noun phrase.</li><li>Wir schicken dem Chef die Nachricht. With two full noun phrases, dative commonly precedes accusative.</li><li>Ich danke meinem Bruder. *Danken* takes dative; masculine dative possessive is *meinem*.</li><li>Sie gibt der Kollegin das Formular. *Geben* needs both recipient (*der Kollegin*, dative) and thing (*das Formular*, accusative).</li></ol></details>

For items 17–28, award one point per correct form and one point per accurate role explanation where requested (maximum 14 points). Treat this as retrieval practice, not a CEFR placement score. If you miss article endings, revisit L04–L05; if you miss object roles or order, revisit L06.

## E. Chapter 3 review (L07–L09)

Correct the location sentence: *Die Schlüssel sind in den Schublade.* 38. Explain the difference: *Ich laufe im Park* / *Ich laufe in den Park*.

Read the short message and answer 39–42.

*Hallo Paul, am Donnerstag muss ich um sieben Uhr losfahren. Ich steige am Rathaus in die Straßenbahn ein und fahre bis zum Bahnhof. Dort warte ich auf dich. Stell dich bitte vor den Eingang; ich komme um Viertel nach sieben an. Danach können wir zusammen in ein Café gehen. Bis dann, Nora.*

39. At what time does Nora leave? 40. Where does she board the tram and where does she get off? 41. Where should Paul wait? 42. What do they plan to do afterward?

<details><summary>Show answers and explanations</summary><ol><li>muss — *ich* form; infinitive *aufstehen* is at the end.</li><li>Sie ruft ihren Bruder nach der Arbeit an. Finite verb second; object before the separated prefix.</li><li>*Steigen Sie bitte an der nächsten Haltestelle aus.* Polite imperative uses *Sie* and separates *aus*.</li><li>*am Dienstag um 15 Uhr* — *am* with weekdays; *um* with clock time.</li><li>*mit dem Bus* — *mit* governs dative.</li><li>*auf den Zug* — *warten auf* takes accusative.</li><li>*unter dem Tisch* — location (*wo?*), dative masculine.</li><li>*unter den Tisch* — destination (*wohin?*), accusative masculine.</li><li>*Die Schlüssel sind in der Schublade.* Feminine dative location uses *der*.</li><li>First is where the running takes place (location, dative); second gives the destination (accusative).</li><li>At 7:00.</li><li>She boards at Rathaus and gets off at the Bahnhof.</li><li>In front of the entrance.</li><li>They plan to go to a café together.</li></ol></details>

For this section, award one point for each correct response (14 points). If you miss 29–31, review L07; 32–34, L08; 35–38, L09. Reading responses 39–42 map to route/time understanding in L08–L09. For explanation 38, accept any clear wording that distinguishes the activity location from its destination.
