## Checks completed on 2026-10-18

- [Duden: Kasus erklärt](https://www.duden.de/sprachwissen/sprachratgeber/kasus-erkl%C3%A4rt-deutsche-f%C3%A4lle-bestimmen) — checked for the four case names, question prompts, and case-function examples used in the case handbook and L04–L06.
- [Duden: Nomen](https://www.duden.de/sprachwissen/fuer-lernende/wortarten-nomen) — checked for article/case paradigms and plural dative formation.
- [Duden: Präpositionen](https://www.duden.de/sprachwissen/fuer-lernende/wortarten-praepositionen) — checked for governed cases, contractions, and the *wo?* (dative) / *wohin?* (accusative) distinction with place prepositions.
- [Duden: Perfekt with haben or sein](https://www.duden.de/sprachwissen/sprachratgeber/Perfektbildung-mit-haben-oder-sein) — checked for the default *haben* pattern, common *sein* classes, and meaning/usage caveats for motion verbs.
- [Duden: strong and weak verbs](https://www.duden.de/sprachwissen/sprachratgeber/Starke-und-schwache-Verben) — checked for weak, strong and mixed participle formation distinctions and principal-part examples.
- [Duden: functions of als](https://www.duden.de/sprachwissen/sprachratgeber/Funktionen-von-als) and [Duden: wenn](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/wenn) — checked for temporal subordinate-clause use, repeated *wenn*, and conditional meaning.

These checks support the case and preposition material in L04–L09 and the case handbook, and the past-tense/temporal-clause material in L10–L12 and the past-narration reference. The other listed references, including current telc format details, remain unverified and must be checked before the corresponding sections are authored.
